IOHANNES / EST. IN IDEA
01 / THE ORIGIN

A NAME WITH
MEANING.

IOHANNES takes its name from a personal story of identity and grace. The brand translates that meaning into an approach to streetwear that values purpose, originality and self-expression.

Starting with the everyday essentials — tees, sweatshirts and sweatpants — the idea is simple: recognisable designs, thoughtful quality and small collections developed with care.

01

CREATIVE FIRST.

Trends can inspire us. They don't define us. Every release begins with an original point of view.

02

QUALITY COUNTS.

Everyday garments should feel worth wearing again and again. Fit and materials matter.

03

LESS, WITH PURPOSE.

Limited quantities keep each release intentional. We aim for interest, not artificial hype.

Good to know.

How do I choose my size?+

The prototype product details show sample sizing information. A real launch would include garment measurements and a complete size guide.

When will the next drop be released?+

There is no live sale or scheduled drop in this university prototype. Future launches would be announced through the website.

How do delivery and returns work?+

Delivery and returns details are illustrative. A real store would publish delivery charges, timeframes and a legally compliant returns policy before accepting orders.

Marketing updates would require an optional opt-in. This prototype does not send emails or collect server-side personal data.
